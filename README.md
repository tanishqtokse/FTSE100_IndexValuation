# 📊 FTSE 100 — Intrinsic Valuation

> **Equity Valuation | FCFE Model | Financial Modelling | Sensitivity Analysis**

A fundamental valuation of the **FTSE 100 Index** designed to estimate its intrinsic value and assess the potential upside/downside relative to its market level.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to determine whether the FTSE 100 is **undervalued, overvalued or fairly Valued** based on fundamental assumptions and an FCFE-based valuation framework.

The analysis focuses on:

* 📈 Intrinsic value estimation
* 💰 Free Cash Flow to Equity
* 📉 Cost of Equity
* 📊 Market-based valuation assumptions
* 🔄 Dividend & share buyback analysis
* 🎯 Terminal value
* 📐 Sensitivity analysis

---

## 🧮 Valuation Approach

### FCFE Valuation

The model estimates the present value of future cash flows available to equity shareholders.

**Core framework:**

```text
Risk Free Rate
  ↓
Equity Risk Premium
  ↓
FTSE100 Historical Calculation
  ↓   
FCFE
  ↓
Forecast Future Cash Flows
  ↓
Discount at Cost of Equity
  ↓
Terminal Value
  ↓
Present Value
  ↓
Intrinsic Value of FTSE 100
```

### Key Inputs

| Valuation Driver          |  Value |
| ------------------------- | -----: |
| FTSE 100 Market Level     | 10937.05 |
| Risk-Free Rate            |    4.75% |
| Equity Risk Premium       |    4.29% |
| Cost of Equity            |    9.04% |
| Estimated Intrinsic Value | 11417.14 |
| Upside                    |    4.38% |

---

## 📌 Key Takeaway

> **Estimated Intrinsic Value:** 11417.14
> **Current Market Level:** 10937.05
> **Potential Upside  :** 4.38%

Based on the assumptions incorporated in the model, the FTSE 100 appears to be **[fairly valued]** relative to its current market level.

## 📊 Sensitivity Analysis

The model includes sensitivity analysis to evaluate how changes in:

* **Cost of Equity**
* **Terminal Growth Rate**

impact the estimated intrinsic value.

This helps assess the robustness of the valuation and highlights the key assumptions driving the investment case.
